# Herald, The Place, and Vera

The canonical concept document is [docs/concept.md](../../docs/concept.md).

This page records the durable wiki-level shape of the system without
duplicating the full source document.

## Concept

Herald, The Place, and Vera are three layers of a protocol for human trust:

- **Herald:** a personal agent that represents one human, learns their values
  and constraints, prepares coordination, and guards against sycophancy drift.
- **The Place:** the coordination platform where Heralds resolve semantic,
  factual, modeling, and value layers before humans spend relational bandwidth.
- **Vera:** the protocol state that defines citizenship, trust ledgers,
  verification, governance, and portability.

## Durable Constraints

- Trust is context-specific, not globally transferable by default.
- Vulnerability trust and capability trust are separate ledgers.
- Bilateral vulnerability health detects extractive disclosure patterns.
- Social mediation is proof of work only when it produces verified real-world
  coordination value.
- The protocol should be governed as public infrastructure, not as an
  engagement product or private reputation database.
- Herald can remove avoidable friction, but it must not replace human
  vulnerability, accountability, or shared experience when those are the point.
